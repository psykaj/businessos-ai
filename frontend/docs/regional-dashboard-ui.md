# Regional Analytics Dashboard UI (`/dashboard/regional-analytics`)

## Recharts Executive Intelligence
The Regional Analytics Command Center fuses multi-branch telemetry into high-impact interactive charts:
1. **Revenue vs. Profit Comparison**: Grouped Recharts Bar Chart contrasting gross top-line performance against net regional profitability margins across continental territories.
2. **Customer Distribution Yield**: Donut Chart charting active client density and measuring employee revenue yield per region.
3. **Branch Growth Momentum vs. Score**: Multi-axis Line Chart correlating Month-over-Month revenue growth trajectories against composite AI performance scores (0-100).
4. **Territory Audit Table**: Clean, sortable data table providing immediate visibility into regional workforce productivity and highlighting the top-performing Champion Branch per continent.

## Business Value
- **Save Executive Time**: Eliminates spreadsheet compilation by synthesizing dozens of branches into single-glance regional insights.
- **Improve Decision-Making**: Instant identification of underperforming operational hubs and high-margin growth regions.
