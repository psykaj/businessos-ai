# Cash Flow Analytics UI Documentation

The Cash Flow Analytics workspace (`/dashboard/cash-flow`) delivers real-time visibility into business liquidity, historical trends, and 30-day cash projections.

## Core Features & Components
1. **30-Day Forward Cash Flow Projection (`CashFlowForecastCard`)**:
   - Projected cash inflows from outstanding Accounts Receivable due in 30 days.
   - Projected cash outflows from Accounts Payable & recurring overhead.
   - Projected net bank position at month end.

2. **Multi-Month Trend Analytics (`CashFlowChart`)**:
   - Recharts area chart visualizing Cash In (Collections) vs Cash Out (Expenses/Bills).

3. **Key Liquidity Metrics**:
   - Total Cash Received, Total Cash Disbursed, Bank Cash Position, Net Cash Flow.
