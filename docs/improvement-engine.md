# AI Improvement Recommendation Engine

## The Ultimate Business Value Multiplier
While metrics and dashboards show *what* is happening, the **AI Improvement Recommendation Engine** tells business owners exactly **what to do next** to maximize ROI:
* **Save Time**: Generates concrete operational action plans automatically without requiring consulting analysis or meetings.
* **Increase Revenue & Protect ARR**: Calculates the exact financial dollar impact ($) associated with every recommended retention or upsell action.
* **Reduce Costs**: Pinpoints recurring product flaws and onboarding bottlenecks that generate unnecessary customer support ticket overhead.

---

## Recommendation Categories & Action Strategies

The engine categorizes opportunities into 5 core growth imperatives:

1. **Contact Unhappy Customers** (`Urgent Priority`)
   * *Trigger*: Detection of severe detractors, cancellation words, or critical billing complaints.
   * *Business Impact*: Quantifies and protects Annual Recurring Revenue ($ ARR) at immediate churn risk.
2. **Improve Response Time** (`High Priority`)
   * *Trigger*: SLA response delays exceeding first-contact targets during peak business volume.
   * *Business Impact*: Recommends automated AI triage workflows to save agent hours and lift CSAT.
3. **Reward Loyal Customers** (`Medium Priority`)
   * *Trigger*: Identification of persistent brand promoters giving continuous 5-star ratings without active referral incentives.
   * *Business Impact*: Triggers VIP partner referral commissions to drive organic revenue pipeline growth.
4. **Follow Up After Purchase** (`Medium Priority`)
   * *Trigger*: Newly acquired accounts lacking structured feedback capture during initial onboarding milestones.
   * *Business Impact*: Preemptively prevents early 60-day customer drop-off and friction.
5. **Improve Product Quality** (`High Priority`)
   * *Trigger*: Clusters of similar sentiment complaint themes regarding navigation, reporting, or product features.
   * *Business Impact*: Eliminates recurring routine support tickets, directly reducing technical overhead costs.

---

## Continuous Background Discovery
The `RecommendationDiscoveryWorker` continuously scans feedback volume, SLA metrics, and customer churn scores in the background to refine and generate new high-ROI business recommendations.
