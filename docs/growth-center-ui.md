# AI Growth Center Master Orchestrator Technical Guide

## Overview
The AI Growth Center Master Orchestrator (`/dashboard/growth-center`) operates as an autonomous digital strategy consultant within BusinessOS AI. Rather than generating complex data dumps, it synthesizes telemetry from 8 core backend modules (Revenue, Marketing, Products, Customers, Accounting, Inventory, Workflows, and Communication) into a prioritized feed of actionable growth recommendations.

---

## Algorithmic Prioritization & Opportunity Vault
Every recommendation generated by the backend Growth Intelligence extensions is assigned three critical numeric vectors:
1. **Estimated Revenue Impact ($/yr)**: Total projected annual recurring revenue expansion.
2. **Cost Reduction Impact ($/yr)**: Direct operational or waste reduction savings.
3. **AI Confidence Factor (0-100%)**: Probabilistic score based on historical conversions and market elasticity models.

### Opportunity Vault Scorecard
At the apex of the Growth Center view, the system presents an executive scorecard summing all open financial opportunities into a single **Total Available Financial Yield** (e.g. `$134,640 / year`), splitting attribution into Revenue Expansion versus Operational Savings.

---

## Strategy Categories & Action Execution Vectors
The interface supports filtering and executing actions across 5 dedicated strategic vectors:

### 1. Campaign Optimization
- **Detected Pattern**: Broad display networks generating sub-2.0x ROAS while LinkedIn Executive ABM achieves >5.2x ROAS with low CAC.
- **Automated Workflow**: Instantly adjusts ad network API budget allocation ($8,500/mo shifted to LinkedIn ABM), yielding an estimated `+$44,540/yr ARR Lift`.

### 2. Inventory Reorder & Reserve Management
- **Detected Pattern**: Dedicated API Gateway reserves drop below safety limits prior to seasonal enterprise billing surges.
- **Automated Workflow**: Authorizes automated purchase order for 50 reserve cluster licenses from cloud vendors to prevent fulfillment bottlenecks (`+$24,000/yr revenue protected`).

### 3. VIP Customer Retention & Win-Back
- **Detected Pattern**: High-value enterprise logos ($3,050 combined MRR) exhibit portal dormancy >50 days with declining API requests.
- **Automated Workflow**: Triggers automated executive win-back sequence and schedules priority Customer Success intervention calls (`+$36,600/yr saved ARR`).

### 4. Enterprise Upsell & Expansion
- **Detected Pattern**: Accounts utilizing >94% of active workflow execution limits while scaling internal seat count by 40%.
- **Automated Workflow**: Prepares and transmits one-click Stripe discount acceptance link for the Dedicated AI Agent Tool Registry package (`+$18,000/yr MRR lift`).

### 5. Loss Center Elimination
- **Detected Pattern**: Legacy custom scripting tier runs at -20% gross profit margin due to intense manual engineer support overhead.
- **Automated Workflow**: Sunsets legacy SKU immediately, transitioning existing accounts to automated AI Copilot tiers (`+$19,200/yr cost savings captured`).
