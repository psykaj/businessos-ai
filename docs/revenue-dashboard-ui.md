# Revenue & Profitability Analytics Engine Technical Reference

## Architectural Design & Audit-Grade Telemetry
The Revenue & Profitability Analytics module (`/dashboard/revenue-analytics`) gives financial officers and executive teams deep visibility into subscription economics and unit margins. It strictly implements **MRR Component Decomposition**, ensuring every dollar of subscription change is explicitly accounted for across four distinct accounting buckets.

---

## MRR Component Decomposition Formulas
To maintain audit-grade compliance and prevent financial blindness, recurring revenue transitions between months ($T_{n-1}$ to $T_n$) are reconciled via:

$$\text{Ending MRR}_{T_n} = \text{Beginning MRR}_{T_n} + \text{New MRR} + \text{Expansion MRR} - \text{Contraction MRR} - \text{Churned MRR}$$

1. **New MRR**: First-time subscription commitments generated by net-new customer logos acquired within the billing period.
2. **Expansion MRR**: Incremental upgrade recurring fees from existing accounts (e.g., tier promotions, seat expansions, additional API usage clusters).
3. **Contraction MRR**: Recurring subscription fee reductions resulting from customer tier downgrades or discount reapplications.
4. **Churned MRR**: Complete termination of billing contracts from departing logos.

## UI Components & Interactive Audit Workflows

### 1. Multi-Period Trend Visualizations
The main chart component (`RevenueTrendChart`) supports toggling between an Area chart of aggregate gross revenue versus net operational profit, and a stacked Recharts bar visualization that clearly segregates positive expansion components against negative contraction flows.

### 2. Monthly Evolution Ledger
Below the visual graphics sits an interactive data ledger displaying period-over-period percentage growth, exact dollar expansions, and net profit margins. Each row is fitted with a **Drilldown** action hook.

### 3. Audit Drilldown Dialog & Ledger Export
Clicking "Drilldown" invokes an accessible modal dialog rendering granular insights for the selected monthly billing cycle:
- **Net New MRR Added Summary**: Reconciling ($New + Expansion - Contraction - Churned$).
- **Executive Audit Notes**: Auto-synthesized text detailing which product upgrades drove expansion and confirming successful proactive retention interventions.
- **Ledger Export Hook**: One-click download utility that compiles complete financial tenant records into encrypted CSV reports for external accounting platforms or board review.
