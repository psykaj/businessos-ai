# Executive Communication Analytics UI

## Overview
The Communication Analytics suite (`/dashboard/communication-analytics`) transforms raw conversation interactions into actionable executive intelligence using Recharts visualizations and Redis-cached 15-minute aggregations.

## KPI Modules
- **Response & Resolution Velocity**: Measures average team response times and total resolution hours against SLA business targets.
- **Omnichannel Distribution Chart**: Interactive bar chart displaying message volume split across WhatsApp, Email, Live Chat, and SMS.
- **CSAT Breakdown Pie Chart**: Evaluates customer satisfaction scores (1-5 stars) to identify retention risks.
- **Agent Performance Leaderboard**: Audits team resolution volume, response speeds, and customer ratings to recognize top-performing staff and improve staffing decision-making.
